03

AI & Automation

Automation that removes repeat work, and AI where it genuinely helps.

What we deliver

  • Workflow automation across forms, email, spreadsheets and systems
  • Approval, invoice and notification flows

  • AI assistants for internal or customer tasks

Teams spending hours on manual, repeated tasks that follow clear rules.

Managed Technology

We keep what we build running, backed up and supported, under one name and one point of contact.

What we deliver

  • Managed hosting
  • Database hosting
  • Infrastructure management
  • System maintenance and updates
  • Backups and recovery
  • Deployment and operational support
  • Technical support

Suited to

Businesses that want their systems looked after, without hiring an in-house technical team.

How we work

Understand, build, support.

  1. Understand

    We start by understanding the business, the problem and what needs to change.

  2. Build

    We design and build practical technology and creative solutions around that need.

  3. Support

    We stay involved where ongoing maintenance, infrastructure or creative support is required.
