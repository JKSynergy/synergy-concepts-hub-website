Synergy Creative Hub  /  Social Media Content

Monthly social media content production for businesses.

We plan, shoot and edit the photos, videos and graphics your business needs for social media in one monthly recording session. You post them when it suits you.

How it works

Plan, record, deliver.

  1. Plan

    We agree a monthly content plan around your offers, products and calendar.

  2. Record

    One recording session produces a month of material.

  3. Deliver

    Edited videos and designed graphics, sized for each platform and ready to post.

What you get

Content built for business, not just for likes.

  • Monthly content plan
  • Recording session with lighting, audio and direction
  • Short-form videos for Reels, Shorts and TikTok
  • Graphic designs and promotional flyers
  • Files sized for each platform
