Quotes

Every quote starts with the occasion.

Tell us what you are planning and what you would like covered. We reply within 24 hours with questions or a quote for that coverage.

  1. Tell us the occasion

    An event, a wedding, a Kukyala or an introduction ceremony, or a shoot. Include the date and the location.

  2. Choose the coverage

    Photography, videography or both. Drone coverage can be added to suitable packages where the venue and conditions allow.

  3. Receive a quote

    We confirm the details with you and send a quote for that coverage.
