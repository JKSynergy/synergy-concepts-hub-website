# Synergy Concepts Hub: design direction

**Clean. Clear. Corporate. Purposeful.**  Complex work, simple experience.

## Principles
1. Typography, whitespace, a 12-column grid and hairline rules do the work. Cards are the exception.
2. Every section has one job. One idea per screen, in this order: Hero, Technology, Business Creative, Products, Work, SMA bridge, Contact.
3. Technology is shown as large typographic rows (01 to 04). Creative leans on real photography and design work. Products may use cards. Work is image-led.
4. One primary action everywhere: **Start a Project**.
5. Hybrid editorial/business language: more structured and corporate than SMA, more visual where creative work needs it. SMA keeps its own cardless, educational style.

## System (assets/css/sch.css, about 17 KB, replaces the 163 KB ecosystem.css)
- **Colour (dark site, Oct 2026):** the whole site is dark. Three surfaces carry the section rhythm: navy #0B0F19 (header, hero, work, contact, footer), "paper" #111622 (default sections) and "stone" #171D2A (alternate sections). Text #EEF0F3, secondary text #A3ABB9, hairlines white at 12%. Logo orange #ED8C22 for buttons and marks, #F29A3C for small orange text, blue lifted to #6AB0E8. Client logos keep their white square frames. No gradients, glows, glass or blur.
- **Type:** Sora (headings) and Inter (body). Space Grotesk removed. One scale, tight tracking on display sizes.
- **Shape:** 2px corners, 1px rules, no shadows.
- **Rhythm:** sections alternate white, stone and navy only when the content changes character (navy for Hero, Work and Contact).
- **Motion:** colour and underline transitions only. No scroll animation, counters, parallax or tilt. Reduced-motion respected.
- **Components:** `.rows` (typographic list), `.row--img` (row with photo), `.svc` (service block), `.split` (image and text), `.steps`, `.cards` (products only), `.wgrid` (work), `.table`, `.field` (underlined form fields).
- **Portfolio (Oct 2026):** `.gal` (native-ratio gallery, 2/3/4 columns, never cropped; logos sit in square frames, contained), `.jrow`/`.ji` (homepage Selected Work: justified pairs at native proportions), `.gsec` (one gallery section per category, with its own enquiry link), `.jump` (in-page category links), `.duo` (two-column comparison), `.pend` (PENDING marker, review preview only). Full-size viewer: a plain `<dialog>` built by `sch.js`; keyboard arrows, Escape closes, focus returns to the image that opened it. Without JS, gallery links open the image file.

## Removed from the old design
Dashboard mock-ups and fake live data, glass cards (14 on the homepage), 25 pill badges, 138 gradients, 76 blur filters, 39 keyframe animations, animated counters, the "Ecosystem" section, the infrastructure map overlay, per-pillar colour coding, and Space Grotesk.

## Files
`build.py` generates every page (edit copy there, then run `python build.py`). Portfolio items come from `_review/portfolio.json`; run `python build_images.py` after editing it, and `python build.py --preview` for the review copy in `_review/preview/`. Pages live in folders (`/technology/`, `/creative/` and so on). `_redirects` is generated by `build.py` (`REDIRECTS`); the build refuses to write a redirect whose target is not a built page or whose source is still a page.

## Information architecture (Oct 2026): four business destinations
Synergy Concepts Hub is the parent brand; the primary navigation is its four businesses, then Our Work and About.
Each business has a section bar (secondary navigation, sticky under the header on desktop). Configure both in `BUSINESSES` in `build.py`; a page joins a business with `page(..., biz="films")`.

| Business | Section root | Pages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Synergy Concepts Hub (technology, products) | `/technology/` | `/technology/`, `/products/` |
| Synergy Capture Films | `/capture-films/` | `events/`, `weddings/` (weddings, Kukyala, introductions), `photography/` (portraits, studio, product), `videography/`, `quote/` |
| Synergy Creative Hub | `/creative-hub/` | `design/`, `social-media-content/`, `brief/` |
| Synergy Multimedia Academy | `/academy/` | Bridge page; courses, intakes and applications stay on the Academy website |
| Shared | | `/`, `/work/`, `/about/`, `/contact/` |

- `/capture-films/quote/` is the canonical home of the future Capture Films package builder (event type, photography and/or videography, package, optional extras such as drone). Not built: today it is the shared enquiry form limited to Capture Films services. No packages or prices are shown. Drone coverage appears only as an optional inclusion in suitable packages (`DRONE_NOTE`).
- Academy links go only to the verified pages in `SMA_PAGES` in `build.py`.
- Old `/creative/...` URLs 301-redirect to their new pages. Generated folders of moved pages are removed at build time (`RETIRED_OUTPUT_DIRS`).
- Attribution: every enquiry option carries `data-business`; `sch.js` writes `Business: <name>` and `Service: <label>` at the top of the brief sent to the portal's `/api/quote`. The `service` values sent to the portal are unchanged. `<body data-business>` marks each page's business (`sch-group` for shared pages) for future analytics; no analytics runs on the site today.
- New components: `.secbar` (section bar), `.biz` / `.bizlist` (homepage business destinations), `.vh` (visually hidden text).
