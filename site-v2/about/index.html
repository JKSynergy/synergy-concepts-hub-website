About Synergy Concepts Hub in Kampala, Uganda.

Synergy Concepts Hub is a Kampala-based technology and business creative company building software systems, websites, photography, design and digital products for growing organisations.

Structure

One company across technology, creative and education.

01Synergy Concepts HubThe main consolidated company for business technology, creative production and proprietary software products such as QuickCredit and SMA Sync.
02Synergy Capture FilmsOur commercial photography and video production division: corporate portraits, studio sessions, product photography, weddings and business video.→ 03Synergy Creative HubOur graphic design and branding division: logos, brand identity systems, company profiles, presentations and promotional collateral.→ 04Synergy Multimedia AcademyOur dedicated education brand offering practical courses, holiday programs and multimedia training on its own website.↗

How we work

Understand, build, support.

  1. Understand

    We start by understanding the business, the operational or creative need, and what needs to change.

  2. Build

    We design, photograph and build practical software, websites and visual work around that need.

  3. Support

    We stay involved where ongoing maintenance, managed hosting or recurring creative production is required.
