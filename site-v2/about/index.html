About

About Synergy Concepts Hub in Kampala, Uganda.

Synergy Concepts Hub is a Kampala-based technology and business creative company building software systems, websites, photography, design and digital products for growing organisations.

Structure

One parent company, four Synergy businesses.

01Synergy Concepts HubThe parent company, and the home of our business technology and proprietary software products such as QuickCredit and SMA Sync.→ 02Synergy Capture FilmsOur photography and video business: corporate portraits, studio sessions, product photography, weddings and ceremonies, and business video.→ 03Synergy Creative HubOur design and creative production business: logos, brand identity, company profiles, promotional design, social media content and campaigns.→ 04Synergy Multimedia AcademyOur education business, offering practical courses, holiday programs and multimedia training. Admissions run on the Academy’s own website.→

How we work

Understand, build, support.

  1. Understand

    We start by understanding the business, the operational or creative need, and what needs to change.

  2. Build

    We design, photograph and build practical software, websites and visual work around that need.

  3. Support

    We stay involved where ongoing maintenance, managed hosting or recurring creative production is required.
